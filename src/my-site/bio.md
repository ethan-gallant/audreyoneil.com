Audrey O’Neil, born in 2002 in London, Ontario, is a Canadian contemporary ceramicist who recently graduated from NSCAD University with a Bachelor of Fine Arts, Major in Ceramics. She challenges the boundaries of the ceramic medium by fusing science, technology and handcrafted tradition. Inspired by the 17th-century Wunderkammer (Cabinet of Curiosities), her otherworldly creatures and objects describe life and nature in an alternate dimension, exploring themes of curiosity, collection, mortality, and oddities.

O’Neil strives to tell stories of life in another world through objects that were collected, hunted and preserved by an interdimensional traveller. She creates sculptural ceramics through hand-building by pinching clay and solid sculpting, incorporating a variety of textures. Using vibrant and electric colours, her pieces have an alluring effect, making these relics appear otherworldly. O’Neil’s studio practice combines ceramics with textiles, wood, wire, and plastic to create visual contrast that cannot be depicted solely through clay and glaze. Incorporating found objects and materials creates layers of vitality in her work as she attempts to capture memories and past lives of artefacts, as they have encapsulated people, places, and time. O’Neil’s work intersects opposing themes, blending 16th and 17th-century European ornamentation, collection and curation with science fiction.
